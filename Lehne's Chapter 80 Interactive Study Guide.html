Asthma vs COPD - The Big Comparison
Know the differences - HIGH YIELD!
Feature Asthma COPD
Reversibility ✅ REVERSIBLE ❌ IRREVERSIBLE
Inflammation Type Eosinophilic, IgE-mediated Neutrophilic, Macrophage-driven
Structural Change Minimal early Permanent alveolar destruction
Hyperreactivity MARKED Minimal
Steroid Response ✅ EXCELLENT ⚠️ Limited
Smoking Trigger, not cause PRIMARY CAUSE
Primary Goal Prevent inflammation & attacks Reduce symptoms & exacerbations
📋 NCLEX Exam Tip!
If the stem mentions:
"Child," "Allergy," "Reversible," "Nighttime symptoms" → ASTHMA

"Smoker," "Productive cough," "Air trapping," "Poor exercise tolerance" → COPD

Asthma Inflammatory Cascade
Step-by-Step - Why the drugs work!
▼
1
Allergen binds IgE on mast cells
Triggers the whole cascade
2
Mast Cell Degranulation
Releases: Histamine → Bronchoconstriction | Leukotrienes → Edema, Mucus | Prostaglandins → Inflammation
3
Eosinophil Recruitment
More inflammation cells arrive
4
Chronic Inflammation
Mucus plugging → Smooth muscle hypertrophy → Bronchial hyperreactivity
Drug Tie-In - Where They Work!
STEROIDS → Stop steps 3-4 (inflammation & remodeling)
Leukotriene Modifiers → Block step 2 (leukotrienes)
β₂ Agonists → Reverse bronchospasm only (step 2 effect)

This explains why SABAs alone eventually FAIL!
🫁
COPD Pathophysiology
Why bronchodilators can't fix it all
▼
⚙️ The Destructive Process
  • Chronic exposure → Protease > Antiprotease
  • Loss of elastin → AIR TRAPPING
  • ↓ Alveolar surface area → HYPOXEMIA
  • Hypercapnia develops later
📌 Key Concept
Bronchodilators help airflow, BUT...
Alveoli are DESTROYED → No full reversal possible!
💉
Glucocorticoids - Foundation of Asthma!
The MOST important drug class
▼
⚙️ Mechanism (How They Work)
  • ↓ Cytokine gene transcription
  • ↓ Eosinophil survival
  • ↓ Vascular permeability
  • ↑ β₂ receptor expression → SABAs work BETTER!
Feature Inhaled (ICS) Oral
Role FIRST-LINE Reserved for severe
Effects Local Systemic
Adrenal Suppression Low HIGH
Usage Daily control Short bursts preferred
🚨 Exam Safety Alert!
Oral steroids = STRESS-DOSE needed during illness/surgery!
Adrenal suppression can cause crisis if stopped abruptly.
🦷 ICS Side Effects - Teach Patients!
  • Oral candidiasis (thrush) - RINSE MOUTH after use!
  • Hoarseness/dysphonia
  • Use spacer with MDI to reduce oropharyngeal deposition
⚡
SABAs (Short-Acting β₂ Agonists)
The RESCUE Inhaler - Albuterol!
▼
⚡ Quick Facts
Onset: MINUTES!
Duration: 3-5 hours
Use: PRN (as needed) only
Mechanism
β₂ stimulation → Bronchial smooth muscle RELAXATION →
BRONCHODILATION (opens airways fast!)
🚨 RED FLAG!
Using SABA >2 days/week = STEP THERAPY UP!

This means asthma is NOT controlled!
⚠️ Side Effects
  • Tachycardia
  • Tremor
  • Hypokalemia (at high doses)
🕐
LABAs (Long-Acting β₂ Agonists)
Salmeterol, Formoterol - NEVER Alone in Asthma!
▼
⚡ Quick Facts
Duration: 12-24 hours
Use: PREVENTION only - scheduled dosing
☠️ BLACK BOX WARNING!
LABA MONOTHERAPY IN ASTHMA = INCREASED DEATH RISK!

NEVER use LABA alone in asthma!
Always combine with ICS (e.g., Advair, Symbicort)
✅ COPD Exception
LABA alone IS allowed in COPD!
(Different pathophysiology - not IgE-mediated)
🚫
Anticholinergics (Muscarinic Antagonists)
Ipratropium, Tiotropium - Better in COPD!
▼
⚙️ Mechanism
Block PARASYMPATHETIC tone →
↓ Bronchoconstriction, ↓ Mucus secretion
Drug Type Key Use
Ipratropium SAMA (short-acting) Acute exacerbations + SABA
Tiotropium LAMA (long-acting) COPD maintenance
📌 Key Points
  • More effective in COPD than asthma
  • ADDITIVE with β₂ agonists
  • Side effects: Dry mouth, urinary retention
⚠️
Theophylline - Know WHY It's Dangerous!
Narrow therapeutic index - Rarely first-line!
▼
⚙️ Mechanism
Phosphodiesterase inhibitor → ↑ cAMP → Bronchodilation
🚨 NARROW THERAPEUTIC INDEX!
Affected by MANY factors:
  • Smoking (↑ metabolism)
  • Fever
  • Liver disease
  • MANY drug interactions!
☠️ Toxicity Signs
SEIZURES + DYSRHYTHMIAS
Also: N/V, insomnia, restlessness

Monitor serum levels! (5-15 mcg/mL therapeutic)
📋 NCLEX Pearl
Rarely first-line today due to toxicity risk!
If patient on theophylline has N/V → CHECK LEVEL!

Leukotriene Modifiers
Montelukast (Singulair) - Add-on Therapy
▼
✅ When They're Used
  • Cannot tolerate inhaled steroids
  • Add-on therapy
  • Asthma + Allergic rhinitis (two birds, one stone!)
  • Exercise-induced bronchospasm
❌ Why They're INFERIOR to ICS
Target ONE pathway only (leukotrienes)
vs.
Steroids target MULTIPLE inflammatory pathways!
⚠️ Black Box Concept
Neuropsychiatric effects:
Mood changes, Agitation, Depression, Suicidal thoughts

→ STOP drug if severe!
🛡️
Cromolyn - The "Safest" Drug
Mast Cell Stabilizer - Fallen Out of Favor
▼
⚙️ Mechanism
Stabilizes mast cells → Prevents degranulation →
↓ Histamine & leukotriene release
❌ Why It's Fallen Out of Favor
  • Must be taken BEFORE exposure
  • Takes WEEKS for full effect
  • No bronchodilation
  • No rescue ability
📋 Exam Clue!
"Safest asthma drug" or "prophylactic only" → CROMOLYN
🧪
Monoclonal Antibodies - Severe Asthma Only!
Omalizumab, Dupilumab, etc.
▼
Phenotype Drug Class Target
Allergic asthma Omalizumab Anti-IgE
Eosinophilic asthma Mepolizumab, etc. Anti-IL-5
Steroid-dependent Dupilumab Anti-IL-4/13
Refractory severe Tezepelumab Anti-TSLP
🚨 Always Remember!
  • Given SubQ or IV
  • NOT rescue medications!
  • EXPENSIVE
  • Observe for ANAPHYLAXIS after injection!
🫁
Roflumilast (PDE-4 Inhibitor)
COPD Only - NOT for Asthma!
▼
⚙️ Why NOT Asthma?
Targets NEUTROPHIL inflammation
Asthma is EOSINOPHILIC
Therefore → INEFFECTIVE in asthma!
⚠️ Avoid In:
  • Depression
  • Weight loss (causes more weight loss)

Acute Asthma Exacerbation - Treatment Order!
Know WHY this order matters
1
🫁 OXYGEN
Correct hypoxemia FIRST - patient is dying!
2
⚡ SABA (Albuterol)
Rapid bronchodilation - open the airways!
3
🚫 IPRATROPIUM
Additive bronchodilation effect with SABA
4
💉 SYSTEMIC STEROIDS
Prevent rebound inflammation - takes hours to work!
5
✨ MAGNESIUM SULFATE
Smooth muscle relaxation - for severe/refractory cases

COPD Management - Clinical Logic
Different approach than asthma!
▼
📋 COPD Treatment Order
  • LABA + LAMA FIRST (bronchodilators = mainstay!)
  • Add ICS ONLY if frequent exacerbations
  • Avoid unnecessary steroids
🚨 O₂ in COPD - CAREFUL!
Target SpO₂: 88-92%

Too much O₂ can suppress hypoxic drive →
CO₂ retention → Respiratory failure!
📋 COPD vs Asthma - Key Difference
Asthma: ICS is FOUNDATION
COPD: Bronchodilators are FOUNDATION

(Steroids have limited use in COPD - mostly for exacerbations)

Asthma Step Therapy - The Logic
Step based on severity, adjust based on control
▼
📋 Two Assessment Domains
1. IMPAIRMENT = Symptoms NOW (frequency, nighttime, activity limitation)
2. RISK = Future exacerbations (history, lung function)
Exam Pearl!
Patient may have LOW symptoms but HIGH risk!

Example: Well-controlled on meds but history of ICU admission →
Still needs aggressive therapy!
📈 Step Up / Step Down Rules
Step UP if: Not controlled, using SABA >2 days/week
Step DOWN if: Well-controlled for ≥3 months

🧠 High-Yield Memory Hooks

Asthma = Inflammation
→ STEROIDS are foundation!
COPD = Obstruction
→ BRONCHODILATORS are foundation!
LABA Alone KILLS Asthmatics
Black Box Warning! Always combine with ICS
SABA Overuse = Failure of Control
>2 days/week → Step therapy UP!
ICS ≠ Rescue
Maintenance only! SABA = rescue
Systemic Steroids = TAPER
Never stop abruptly! Adrenal suppression risk

Common Combination Products
Know what's in them!
▼
Brand Name Components Use
Advair Fluticasone + Salmeterol (ICS + LABA) Asthma/COPD
Symbicort Budesonide + Formoterol (ICS + LABA) Asthma/COPD
Combivent Albuterol + Ipratropium (SABA + SAMA) COPD
Anoro Vilanterol + Umeclidinium (LABA + LAMA) COPD
✅ Why Combinations Work
ICS + LABA: Inflammation + Bronchospasm → Better adherence, ↓ exacerbations
LABA + LAMA: Different pathways → Bronchi + Bronchioles dilation → Improved airflow
