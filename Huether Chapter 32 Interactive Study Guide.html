Urinary Tract Obstruction
Obstructive Uropathy - Blockage Anywhere!
▼
📌 Definition
Structural or functional blockage of urine flow anywhere from kidney → urethra
📊 Severity Depends On (5 Factors)
  • Location of obstruction
  • Unilateral vs Bilateral
  • Partial vs Complete
  • Duration
  • Nature of lesion
🚨 Key Consequences
• ↑ Back pressure → ↓ GFR
• ↑ Infection risk
• PERMANENT DAMAGE if prolonged!
🔼
Upper Urinary Tract Obstruction
Kidney & Ureter Level
▼
🎯 Causes
  • Stones
  • Tumors
  • Strictures
  • Congenital compression
  • Retroperitoneal fibrosis
⚙️ Pathophysiology Chain
Urine backs up → ↑ Bowman space pressure →
↓ Glomerular filtration → Leads to:
  • HYDROURETER (dilated ureter)
  • HYDRONEPHROSIS (dilated kidney)
  • Tubulointerstitial fibrosis
Key Test Pearl!
UNILATERAL obstruction → Compensatory hypertrophy of OTHER kidney

BILATERAL obstruction → ANURIA! (<50 mL/day)
💧
Postobstructive Diuresis
Watch Out After Relief!
▼
📌 What Is It?
Sudden LARGE urine output after relief of obstruction
⚠️ Complications
• Dehydration
• Electrolyte imbalances

HIGH RISK in chronic bilateral obstruction!

Kidney Stones (Nephrolithiasis)
The Painful Crystals!
▼
Stone Type Percentage pH Association
Calcium oxalate/phosphate 70-80% Alkaline ↑ CaPO₄
Struvite 5-10% Alkaline (urease bacteria)
Uric acid 5-10% Acidic
Cystine Rare Acidic
Most Common = CALCIUM! (70-80%)
Remember: Calcium is Common!
⚙️ Stone Formation Requires
  1. Supersaturation
  2. Crystallization
  3. Aggregation
  4. ↓ Inhibitors (e.g., uromodulin)
⚠️ Risk Factors
  • Dehydration (most important!)
  • UTIs
  • Obesity, Diabetes
  • High sodium diet
  • Low fluid intake
😵
Clinical Presentation
RENAL COLIC - The Worst Pain!
▼
🩺 Classic Symptoms
  • RENAL COLIC - Severe flank → groin pain (colicky!)
  • Hematuria
  • Nausea/Vomiting
  • Urinary urgency (if distal stone)
📏 Size Matters!
<5 mm → ~50% pass spontaneously
≥1 cm → Unlikely to pass - needs intervention!
🚨 UROLOGIC EMERGENCY!
Obstructing stone + UTI = EMERGENCY!

Requires:
• Immediate decompression
• Antibiotics
• Stone removal

Lower Urinary Tract Obstruction
Below the Kidneys
▼
🎯 Causes
  • Urethral stricture
  • Prostate enlargement (very common!)
  • Pelvic organ prolapse
  • Tumors
  • Neurogenic bladder
⚙️ Bladder Response to Chronic Obstruction
  1. ↑ Detrusor contraction (trying to push past obstruction)
  2. Collagen deposition → TRABECULATION
  3. ↓ Bladder compliance
  4. Urine retention
  5. Hydronephrosis → Renal damage!
🔁
Overactive vs Underactive Bladder
OAB vs UAB
▼
Feature OAB (Overactive) UAB (Underactive)
Symptoms Urgency, Frequency, Nocturia Weak stream, Hesitancy
Mechanism Detrusor OVERACTIVITY Incomplete emptying
Cause Involuntary contractions Often NEUROLOGIC
Treatment Antimuscarinics, β3-agonists, Botox Urodynamic testing needed
🧠
Neurogenic Bladder
VERY TESTABLE - Know the Lesion Levels!
▼
Lesion Location Result Think...
ABOVE pontine center Detrusor hyperreflexia Stroke, Brain injury
C2 - S1 Detrusor-sphincter DYSSYNERGIA Spinal cord injury
BELOW S1 ATONIC bladder (retention + overflow) Cauda equina, Diabetes
Memory Trick!
High lesion = Hyper (overactive)
Low lesion = Lazy (atonic/underactive)

Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs)
E. coli is the STAR!
▼
🦠 Most Common Pathogen
E. COLI (from gut flora - ascending infection!)
🛡️ Host Defenses
  • Urine FLOW (washout effect)
  • Acidic pH
  • Uromodulin
  • Sphincters preventing reflux

Cystitis vs Pyelonephritis
Lower vs Upper UTI
▼
Feature Cystitis (Bladder) Pyelonephritis (Kidney)
Location LOWER (bladder) UPPER (kidney)
Fever Often AFEBRILE HIGH FEVER
Pain Suprapubic FLANK PAIN, CVA tenderness
Symptoms Frequency, Urgency, Dysuria Systemic illness
Urine Finding Bacteria, WBCs WBC CASTS (classic!)
WBC Casts = PYELONEPHRITIS!
Casts form in the TUBULES → indicates KIDNEY involvement!

Nephritic vs Nephrotic - HIGH YIELD!
MUST KNOW for exams!
Feature NEPHRITIC NEPHROTIC
Hematuria YES ✓ No
RBC Casts YES ✓ No
Proteinuria Mild >3.5 g/day!
Edema Yes SEVERE
Mechanism INFLAMMATORY PERMEABILITY
Memory Trick!
NephRITIC = "RITIS" = Inflammation → RBC casts, Hematuria

NephROTIC = "ROTIC" = Protein → >3.5g proteinuria, Severe edema

Acute Kidney Injury (AKI)
Sudden Decline in Function!
▼
📋 Defined By
  • ↑ Creatinine
  • ↓ GFR
  • ↓ Urine output
Type Location Cause
PRERENAL Before kidney ↓ Perfusion (hypovolemia, HF, shock)
INTRARENAL Within kidney ATN most common! (ischemia, nephrotoxins)
POSTRENAL After kidney Obstruction
PRE / INTRA / POST
PRE = Problem BEFORE kidney (not enough blood IN)
INTRA = Problem IN the kidney (damage)
POST = Problem AFTER kidney (urine can't get OUT)
🔄
ATN Phases
Acute Tubular Necrosis - 4 Phases
▼
1 Initiation
2 Extension
3 Maintenance
(OLIGURIA)
4 Recovery
(POLYURIA)
📊 Urine Output Definitions
OLIGURIA: <400 mL/day or <20 mL/hr
ANURIA: <50 mL/day (think bilateral obstruction!)
♻️
Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)
Irreversible Progressive Loss
▼
📋 Definition
GFR <60 mL/min/1.73m² for ≥3 MONTHS
🎯 Most Common Causes
  1. DIABETES (#1!)
  2. Hypertension (#2)
  3. Glomerulonephritis
📌 Hallmarks
  • Proteinuria
  • RAAS activation
  • Fibrosis
  • Uremic syndrome
⭐
Systemic Effects of CKD
TEST FAVORITES - Know All of These!
▼
🩸 Anemia
↓ EPO production by kidneys!
🦴 Bone Disease
↓ Vitamin D activation, ↑ Phosphate
⚡ Hyperkalemia
Can't excrete potassium!
🧪 Metabolic Acidosis
Can't excrete H+ or make HCO₃⁻
❤️ CV Disease
#1 cause of death in CKD!
🛡️ Also Affected
  • Immune dysfunction (↑ infection risk)
  • Uremic syndrome (toxin accumulation)
  • Fluid/electrolyte imbalances
🚨 End-Stage Kidney Disease (ESKD)
<10-15% kidney function remaining
Requires DIALYSIS or TRANSPLANT!
