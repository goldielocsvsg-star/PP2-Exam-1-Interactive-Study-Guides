Bone Cells & Remodeling
Who builds, who breaks?
▼
📊 Where Calcium Lives
>98% in BONE!
Bone is DYNAMIC, not static - constantly remodeling
Cell Type Function Memory Trick
OsteoCLASTS BREAK DOWN bone "CLAST = CRASH"
OsteoBLASTS BUILD new bone "BLAST = BUILD"
🔄 Bone Turnover
Trabecular bone: ~25% replaced yearly (fast!)
Cortical bone: ~3% replaced yearly (slow)

Absorption & Excretion
What increases/decreases calcium?
▼
📍 Absorption (Small Intestine)
Only ~1/3 of dietary calcium absorbed normally!

↑ Increased by: Vitamin D, PTH
↓ Decreased by: Glucocorticoids, Fiber, Oxalates, Phytic acid
Factor Effect on Calcium
THIAZIDE diuretics ↑ INCREASE calcium (retain it)
LOOP diuretics (Furosemide) ↓ DECREASE calcium (waste it)
Calcitonin ↓ DECREASE calcium
High sodium intake ↓ DECREASE calcium
Diuretic Calcium Rule!
Thiazides = Think "T" = TRAP calcium (keep it)
Loops = LOSE calcium
🧬
Hormonal Regulation - MEMORIZE THIS!
PTH, Vitamin D, Calcitonin
Hormone Triggered By Effect on Ca²⁺ How?
PTH LOW calcium ↑ RAISES Ca²⁺ ↑ Bone resorption, ↑ Renal reabsorption, ↑ Vit D activation
Vitamin D PTH activation ↑ RAISES Ca²⁺ ↑ Gut absorption, ↑ Bone resorption
Calcitonin HIGH calcium ↓ LOWERS Ca²⁺ ↓ Bone resorption, ↑ Renal excretion
The Golden Mnemonic!
"PTH Pulls calcium UP" ⬆️
"CalciTONin TONES it DOWN" ⬇️
📌 PTH Also...
↓ DECREASES phosphate levels!
(Vitamin D increases BOTH calcium AND phosphate)

Hypercalcemia
"Bones, Stones, Groans, Psychiatric Overtones"
▼
Classic Mnemonic!
"Bones, Stones, Groans, Psychiatric Overtones"

🦴 Bones - Bone pain
🪨 Stones - Kidney stones, polyuria, polydipsia
🤢 Groans - Constipation, nausea
🧠 Psychiatric Overtones - Lethargy, depression
❤️ Plus: Dysrhythmias!
🎯 Causes
  • CANCER (most severe cause!)
  • Hyperparathyroidism
  • Vitamin D toxicity
  • Thiazide diuretics
  • Sarcoidosis
🚨 Treatment of SEVERE Hypercalcemia
  1. IV NORMAL SALINE (first! dilute & flush)
  2. BISPHOSPHONATES
  3. CALCITONIN
  4. LOOP diuretics (only AFTER rehydration!)
  5. Cinacalcet (for hyperparathyroidism)

Hypocalcemia
Neuromuscular Symptoms!
▼
🚨 Symptoms (NEUROMUSCULAR!)
  • TETANY
  • Muscle spasms
  • LARYNGOSPASM (emergency!)
  • Seizures
  • Paresthesias (numbness/tingling)
🎯 Causes
  • PTH deficiency (often post-thyroid surgery!)
  • Vitamin D deficiency
  • CKD
  • Bisphosphonates
  • Magnesium laxatives
💉 Treatment
SEVERE: IV CALCIUM GLUCONATE
MAINTENANCE: Oral calcium + Vitamin D

Key Disease States
Paget, Hypo/Hyperparathyroidism
▼
Disease What Happens Treatment
Paget Disease Excessive bone resorption → abnormal bone, ↑ Alk Phos, bone pain Bisphosphonates
Hypoparathyroidism ↓ PTH (often post-thyroid surgery) → HYPOCALCEMIA Calcium + Vitamin D
Primary Hyperparathyroidism Adenoma → ↑ PTH → HYPERCALCEMIA Surgery (definitive) or Cinacalcet
Secondary Hyperparathyroidism CKD → phosphate retention → ↑ PTH Treat CKD, phosphate binders

Calcium Salts
Oral & IV Calcium
▼
💊 Oral Calcium
Used for: Mild hypocalcemia & supplementation

⚠️ Do NOT exceed 600 mg per dose!
(Absorption limit - taking more is wasted)
⚠️ Drug Interactions!
Calcium ↓ DECREASES absorption of:
• Tetracyclines
• Fluoroquinolones
• Levothyroxine

Separate by 2-4 hours!
💉 IV Calcium - HIGH YIELD!
CALCIUM GLUCONATE = PREFERRED!

• Give SLOW IV (0.5-2 mL/min)
• Monitor for infiltration (tissue necrosis!)

⚠️ DIGOXIN + CALCIUM = BRADYCARDIA!
(Calcium potentiates digoxin toxicity)

Cinacalcet (Sensipar)
Calcimimetic - Lowers PTH!
▼
⚙️ Mechanism
Calcimimetic = Mimics calcium at parathyroid gland →
↓ PTH secretion
✅ Uses
  • Primary hyperparathyroidism
  • Secondary hyperparathyroidism (CKD)
⚠️ Can Cause HYPOCALCEMIA!
Monitor calcium closely when using cinacalcet!

Bisphosphonates - HIGH YIELD!
Alendronate (Fosamax) = Prototype
⚙️ Mechanism
Inhibits OSTEOCLASTS → ↓ Bone resorption
(Remember: OsteoCLAST = CRASH bone)
✅ Indications
  • Osteoporosis (treatment & prevention)
  • Paget disease
  • GIOP (Glucocorticoid-induced osteoporosis)
🏆 NCLEX GOLD - Administration Rules!
  1. Take in the MORNING
  2. Take on EMPTY STOMACH
  3. Take with WATER ONLY (full glass!)
  4. Stay UPRIGHT ≥30 MINUTES
  5. No food, calcium, or iron for 30-60 min
Why Upright 30 Minutes?
Prevents ESOPHAGITIS - the drug is caustic!
Lying down = pill sits in esophagus = BURNS!
⚠️ Adverse Effects
• ESOPHAGITIS (most common!)
• OSTEONECROSIS OF JAW (ONJ)
• Atypical femur fractures
• Ocular inflammation
🐟
Calcitonin (Salmon)
Lowers Calcium - "Tones it Down"
▼
⚙️ Mechanism
↓ Bone resorption
↑ Calcium excretion
✅ Uses
  • Osteoporosis
  • Paget disease
  • Hypercalcemia (not first-line)
⚠️ Side Effects
Nasal irritation common (nasal spray form)
Cancer risk debated
Raloxifene (Evista)
SERM

MOA: Estrogen-like on BONE, Anti-estrogen on breast/uterus

Bonus: Prevents breast cancer!

⚠️ DVT/PE risk!

Teriparatide (Forteo)
PTH Analog

ONLY bone-BUILDING drug!

Daily SubQ injection

⚠️ Osteosarcoma risk (animal studies)

Denosumab (Prolia)
RANKL Inhibitor

MOA: ↓ Osteoclast formation

Must give: Calcium + Vitamin D

⚠️ Hypocalcemia, ONJ, Infections

Teriparatide is SPECIAL!
It's the ONLY drug that BUILDS bone!
All others just SLOW bone loss.

Osteoporosis Essentials
Diagnosis & Prevention
T ≤ -2.5
T-score on DEXA scan = OSTEOPOROSIS
-1 to -2.5 = Osteopenia
🩺 Diagnosis
DEXA scan (Dual-energy X-ray Absorptiometry)
Measures bone mineral density at hip & spine
🛡️ Prevention
  • Calcium + Vitamin D
  • Weight-bearing exercise
  • No smoking
  • Limit alcohol
